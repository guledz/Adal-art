02 — COLLECTIONS

The Heart of ADAL

Six collections form the visual vocabulary of Harar. Each one is an invitation to see the walled city through a different lens — architectural, cultural, symbolic.

01

JUGOL

The walled city, its streets, walls and atmosphere.

02

ARCHITECTURE

Doors, windows, arches, houses, minarets and geometric forms.

03

GATES

The historic entrances and symbolic gateways of Harar.

04

CULTURE

People, clothing, traditions, objects and everyday life.

05

HARAR BLUE

A dedicated visual exploration of Harar's blue identity.

06

ICONS

Coffee, traditional objects, architecture, transportation and recognizable symbols.
